{{ localized.text("For the purposes of the Campaigns page, the MITRE ATT&CK team uses the term Campaign to describe any grouping of intrusion activity conducted over a specific period of time with common targets and objectives. For named Campaigns, the team makes a best effort to track overlapping names, which are designated as “Associated Campaigns” on each page, as we believe these overlaps are useful for analysts.
